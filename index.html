🤔 What is InkDoc?

InkDoc is a free, open-source desktop application that transforms your documents into clean, well-structured Markdown format — the preferred format for AI tools, developers, and content creators. Whether you have a scanned PDF, a complex Word document, or a messy HTML file, InkDoc handles it with ease. Best of all, everything runs 100% locally, meaning your files never leave your computer. No cloud, no uploads, no privacy concerns.

🔧 How InkDoc Works

Using InkDoc is as easy as 1-2-3:

  1. Open InkDoc and drag your document into the main window. You can also click "Browse" to select a file.
  2. Choose your settings — want OCR for scanned documents? Toggle it on. Need tables extracted? InkDoc handles that automatically.
  3. Click "Convert" and watch the magic happen. Your Markdown file appears in seconds, ready to copy or save.

InkDoc uses advanced AI-powered OCR technology (GLM-OCR) to read text from images and scanned documents with incredible accuracy. It also uses state-of-the-art document parsing libraries (MarkItDown, Docling, and Markit) to ensure perfect structure preservation.
